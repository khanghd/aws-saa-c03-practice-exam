Exam overview

MụcThông tin
Format60 questions, 120 phút (~2 phút/câu)
Question typesMultiple-choice (1 đáp án), multiple-response ("Choose TWO"), sequence/ordering (sắp xếp thứ tự)
Passing score720/1000 (scaled score)
LanguageEnglish hoặc Japanese, qua Pearson VUE

Domain 2 + 4 + 6 = 52% → ưu tiên ôn kỹ nhất.

3 golden rules (ba nguyên tắc vàng)

  • Process controls, not instructions to Claude (kiểm soát bằng quy trình, không bằng lời dặn)
  • Independent verification against the source of truth, never self-verification (Claude tự kiểm tra)
  • Proportional to risk (tương xứng rủi ro): không quá lỏng, không quá cực đoan

2. Output Evaluation & Validation 21%

Accuracy, Completeness, Relevance

PillarCâu hỏiLỗi điển hình
AccuracyWrong facts? (sai)Sai số liệu so với nguồn
CompletenessMissing requirements? (thiếu)Thiếu phần; section under-scoped; vi phạm format/negative constraint
RelevanceUnrequested content? (thừa)Filler, preamble, sycophancy, scope creep

Đủ topic nhưng 1 section chỉ cover 1/3 chiều sâu → meets topic-level requirements but under-scoped → expand before delivery. Dùng nguồn chọn lọc, bỏ sót thông tin bất lợi → selective use of sources → re-prompt.

Plausibility vs. verification

Fluent / confident / "well-known" / "sounds authoritative" ≠ đã kiểm chứng. Câu hỏi chuẩn: "Can each specific claim be traced to a credible source or the supplied input?" Unsourced claim → treat as unverified → source nó từ nguồn uy tín hoặc xoá bỏ.

Hallucination & ungrounded extrapolation

Dấu hiệu

  • Fabricated citations, non-existent URLs, invented API/method names, unsourced statistics
  • False premise (chiều theo tiền đề sai trong câu hỏi)
  • Thường do thông tin sau training cutoff mà không có tài liệu đi kèm

Extrapolation (suy diễn ngoài nguồn)

  • Causal leap ("after" → "caused by")
  • Numerical anchoring (thêm số)
  • Scope generalization (mở rộng phạm vi)
  • Presumed intent (gán động cơ)
  • Severity distortion (sai mức nghiêm trọng)

Suspected hallucination — bước đầu tiên luôn là pinpoint the specific unsupported claim.

Prevent & detect (4 bước)

#Kỹ thuậtCách làm
1GroundingXML tags + "use only the provided documents" + citations [DocID: Section]
2Ignorance licensingCho phép "the documents do not contain this information"
3Claim decompositionAtomic propositions → map từng claim với source → Fully Grounded / Partially Grounded / Extrapolated / Contradicted
4Deterministic verificationChạy code, link checker, tra DOI, tính lại bằng spreadsheet hoặc Code Execution

Conflicting sources

Locate primary/authoritative source. Thứ tự ưu tiên: hierarchy of authority → recency → scope specificity. Vẫn không rõ → explicit dissonance reporting + human escalation. Không average, không silent synthesis, "nhiều bài báo lặp lại" (trade press) ≠ primary source.

Logic & math audit

Kiểm tra formulas, units, rounding ở mọi bước; reconcile table vs. text; trace backward. Lỗi nghiêm trọng nhất về clarity: conclusion contradicts the evidence. Đúng nhờ hai lỗi cancel out → vẫn fails the audit.

Bias & fairness

Generalization về nhóm nhân khẩu học → focus on observed behaviors, validate against actual data. Interview questions phải apply equally to all candidates, không giả định về background, chỉ dựa role requirements. "Disclaimer" hay "internal use nên không sao" đều sai.

HITL (Human-in-the-Loop) & escalation

Quantitative triggers (số tiền, số người ảnh hưởng) + qualitative triggers (external-facing, service-level commitment, legal, HR, sensitive data, uncertainty).

audience & impact→ evaluate accuracy/completeness→ compare với escalation criteria→ decide (use/iterate/escalate)→ document rationale

Evaluation checklist: define criteria → set threshold → score → document → promote/forward.

Trap answers: "Are you sure?", rate your own confidence, pick the more confident answer, ask again and take the majority, review only intro & conclusion.

Business process mapping

CategoryĐặc điểmVai trò Claude

Greatest Claude leverage: drafting and synthesizing across many sources for a downstream reviewer. Claude không phải sole decision-maker cho quyết định ảnh hưởng con người/tài chính.

DiscernmentĐánh giá outputSystematic check against requirements, sources, professional standards

Workflow design

  • Integration: document current workflow → identify where Claude augments/humans remain → define inputs/outputs/handoffs → pilot → deploy
  • Prompt chaining: structured intermediate outputs + checkpoints, vì errors propagate downstream
  • Reliable integration: standardize input format + output verification step
  • Material improvement = loại bỏ lặp lại (Project reference) + quality check before downstream; đổi tên/heading chỉ là cosmetic

Decomposition

  • Decompose khi có multiple distinct outputs
  • Thứ tự: define objective → major phases → chi tiết
  • Report nhiều phần: goals → inputs per section → draft từng section → synthesize executive summary last
  • Vendor evaluation: criteria → data → score & rank
  • Research: define research questions first; Solution design: articulate problem & constraints first

Requirements analysis

Synthesize inputs thành requirements with traceability to each source. Conflicting requirements → flag, document each side's source, route back to stakeholders.

gather & label sources→ structured extraction prompt→ group & flag conflicts→ validate→ document

Optimization

Bottleneck đo bằng cycle time per step và queue length.

map workflow→ identify bottleneck→ targeted optimization→ pilot & measure vs. baseline→ roll out

Before changing: measure current quality/time/cost + identify problem steps.

Stakeholder expectation management

Truyền đạt: probabilistic, non-deterministic, realistic gains, known limitations/failure modes. Rủi ro quan trọng nhất cho customer-facing: confident-sounding output with unsupported claims → human review. Executive nói "không cần review" → acknowledge gains + giải thích vì sao human review vẫn essential, kèm ví dụ. Before drafting messages: identify stakeholder groups + define key facts & boundaries.

Trap answers: automate everything, sole decision-maker, one megaprompt, draft summary first, "AI is taking care of it", never makes errors.

5. Configuration & Knowledge Management 12%

Project setup

  • First step: define the purpose and recurring tasks (và boundaries)
  • Custom instructions gồm: role, standards/brand voice, standard structure & length
  • Không đưa: sở thích cá nhân của một người, quy định không liên quan deliverable
  • Instructions phải specific: vd "formal, no contractions", "minimum length", "credit only when policy permits"
  • Skill + knowledge + instructions tham chiếu lẫn nhau

Project knowledge

IssueFix
Prior versions / duplicateKeep only current documents
Stale dataPeriodic review dù output chưa degrade
Noise (email cũ, file khác workstream)Chỉ upload relevant documents

Context bleed & separation

Separate Projects per client engagement, mỗi Project có memory/knowledge/instructions riêng, rồi verify no context bleed.

Maintenance

Output drift → review instructions & reference materials trước tiên. Memory maintenance: edit/delete stale entries, add missing context, export backup before changes. "If it works once, it works forever" → sai, cần periodic review.

Context window

Nên làm

  • Trim irrelevant material, extract only relevant sections
  • Delimiters/section headers cho từng nguồn
  • Tài liệu quan trọng near the beginning or end of context
  • Reuse the same context block thay vì dựng lại

Tránh

  • Paste everything
  • Concatenate without separators
  • Mix sources in one block

Connectors

Before authorizing: approved-vendor list + data classification & permission. Safe: approved scope, read-only public KB. Unsafe: personnel records, payroll, personal cloud storage.

6. Governance, Risk & Responsible Use 15%

Data handling

classify content & identify sensitive fields→ redact→ peer review→ submit

PII

  • Name, email, phone, home address, account number, government identifier, payment-card number
  • Kỹ thuật: redaction, pseudonymization, data minimization (chỉ fields necessary for the task)

Phù hợp vs không

  • Phù hợp: redacted job description, anonymized policy summary
  • Không phù hợp: accommodation notes, employee IDs, salary with names

Policy compliance

Policy expressly prohibits → do not enter, consult leadership for an approved alternative. Không lách bằng: Incognito, paraphrase, chờ hết thời hạn, "internal use", sandbox. Confidential figure → Incognito hoặc disable Memory contribution.

Governance framework

Approved tools, allowed data, required reviews, reporting paths → AI được dùng consistently and accountably.

Use-case assessment

audience, impact, reversibility→ data types / sensitive data→ policies & regulations→ decide (proceed/modify/decline)→ document rationale

Ethics review

audience & impact→ review generalizations/fairness/accuracy→ transparency requirements→ revise or escalate→ record

Greatest ethical concern (HR): quyết định ảnh hưởng việc làm without human accountability; output systematically disadvantage a protected group.

Skills & third-party trust / Prompt injection

Skills: verify source against approved-Skill list + review requested permissions.

Prompt injection: direct (jailbreak) vs. indirect (lệnh ẩn trong web/email/document). Defense: separate instructions from data, treat external content as untrusted, least privilege, human approval before high-impact actions.

Trap answers: instruct Claude not to reference, upload then ask Claude to redact, replace PII with other PII, add PII "for context", sandbox removes the need for review.

7. Troubleshooting & Optimization 10%

Diagnostic sequence

read output→ identify the specific deficiency→ compare output with prompt→ hypothesis→ one targeted change→ record root cause
SymptomRoot causeFix
Vague, off-targetThiếu task, audience, structureClear task + context
Bỏ sót câu hỏiTask statement không enumerateList each question
Inconsistent terminologyThiếu glossaryProvide glossary
Outdated answersStale knowledgeRemove old versions
Missed detailsContext overloadNarrow context
Drift over long chatContext driftSummary + fresh chat
Unsupported claimsHallucinationGrounding + verification
Simple task slow/costlyModel quá lớnSmaller model

Iteration rules

  • One identifiable change per iteration, capture change and effect
  • Mỗi change nhắm vào specific diagnosed weakness; compare against original criteria
  • Output ngày càng lệch template → revert to the closest version và chỉnh từ baseline
  • Plateau → diminishing returns → human review hoặc different approach
  • Stakeholder nêu 2 concern → address only those two, giữ nguyên phần đã duyệt, document changes

Feedback loop

record specific feedback→ 1–2 focused adjustments→ run & compare→ document→ decide (iterate/change/escalate)

Feedback mơ hồ → ask clarifying questions; map each feedback to the change made.

Trap answers: rewrite from scratch, change everything at once, resubmit until it looks right, broad rewrites, aggregate score only.

Sequence questions — mẹo

  • Bước đầu tiên gần như luôn là: define purpose/goal/criteria/research questions, hoặc identify audience & impact
  • Bước cuối gần như luôn là: document/record rationale, hoặc roll out/deploy
  • Bước áp chót thường là: decide (use/iterate/escalate)
  • Pilot luôn đứng trước roll out; measure luôn đứng trước change
  • Executive summary viết sau cùng

"BEFORE" questions (Choose TWO)

Chọn hai bước thuộc giai đoạn chuẩn bị: define, identify, classify, confirm, measure. Loại các bước sau đó: deploy, roll out, promote, archive, send, notify leadership.

Time management

  • First pass ~90 phút; quá 3 phút/câu → chọn tạm, flag, đi tiếp
  • ~30 phút quay lại câu đã flag
  • Chỉ đổi đáp án khi thấy concrete fact you missed

Reading long scenarios

  • Read the question stem first, rồi mới đọc bối cảnh
  • Tìm constraints (sensitive data, policy, volume), goals (consistency, accuracy, speed), qualifiers (first, best, before)
  • Don't add facts mà đề không cho
  • Chọn đáp án addresses all requirements, không chọn đáp án partially correct

3 câu hỏi trước khi chốt đáp án

  • Does it address all requirements?
  • Is risk controlled by process, or only by instructions to Claude?
  • Is it proportional to the risk?
